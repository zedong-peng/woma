# Paper search

Turn a question into explicit search concepts, synonyms, exclusions, and a practical date range. Search more than one relevant source when tools allow it.

For every important claim, preserve the title, authors, year, stable URL or identifier, and the evidence that supports the claim. Separate facts stated by a source from your own inference.

Cluster results by method and limitation rather than returning an unranked list. Identify disagreements, missing comparisons, and assumptions that remain unsupported.

Return:

- the search query and scope;
- a ranked evidence table;
- the closest related approaches;
- limitations that create room for a new idea;
- unresolved questions and search gaps.
